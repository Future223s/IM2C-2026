How the model works

peak = μ + δ
μ = mean of the 3 previous years
δ = Σ wₖ · (xₖ − normalₖ)

δ uses five weather anomalies, all known before the typical peak, with weights shared by every location. For past years, the weights are refit without that year (an honest test). The interval is the 80% range of real out-of-sample errors.

Read before quoting a date
  • Dates are the MODIS mid-greendown satellite proxy: the day forest greenness is halfway through its autumn decline, over a 40 km region. People usually report peak color about 1–3 weeks later. That offset is an assumption and has not been calibrated against news reports.
  • For 2026 there is no actual date yet; the 2026 MODIS product has not been released. 2026 weather runs through .
  • Past-year predictions are honest hindcasts: μ uses only earlier years and the δ weights exclude the year shown. 2001 has no μ because there are no prior years.
